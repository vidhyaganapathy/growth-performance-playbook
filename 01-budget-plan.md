# 01 · Budget plan

## Three budgets, one view

Paid ads, influencer marketing and whitelisting each get their **own budget line**, so one doesn't quietly eat another. All three are judged together in one pipeline report.

| Budget | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Paid ads** | Media spend on Meta, LinkedIn and Google |
| **Influencer** | Creator fees for content and organic posting |
| **Whitelisting** | Paid usage fees + media spend behind creator content |

## Split by role, not by habit

| Bucket | Share (starting point) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Proven** | ~70% | Campaigns already hitting target cost per qualified lead |
| **Scaling** | ~20% | Promising tests getting more budget |
| **Testing** | ~10% | New channels, formats, creators and angles |

Adjust the split to the stage: a brand-new account spends more on testing; a mature one protects the proven bucket.

## Pacing

- Check spend against plan **daily** (or let a pacing agent flag it)
- Flag anything more than ~20% over or under plan
- Re-allocate monthly from what's not working to what is

## In-house vs client budgets

- **In-house:** I plan the budget and get it approved before spend.
- **Client:** I work within the client's set budget and report against it.
